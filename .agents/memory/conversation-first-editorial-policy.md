---
name: Conversation-first parenting consultation
description: User-directed shift from material-selection forms to clearly labeled editorial conversation samples.
---

The user-facing goal is to answer clearly within the evidence and, where needed,
guide the parent toward human support—not require parents to search or compare
materials. Pause complex form refinements unless separately requested.

Understanding a concrete difficulty and thinking together is the primary goal;
professional routing is not the default endpoint of every authored conversation.

**Why:** The user rejected uniform question sequences and referral endings even
when the handoff mechanics worked. Administrative correctness alone did not
demonstrate a responsive consultation.

**How to apply:** Follow the person's last reply into a specific barrier or
episode before asking generic history. Leave room for open answers instead of
forcing feelings versus practical-support choices. Vary authored lengths and
question order, include meaningful reflections, and permit a conversation to
continue without handoff. Record missing intervention evidence for reviewers,
not invented advice for parents. Keep credentials/sharing explanations in
operating disclosures rather than repeating them in conversational replies.

**Why:** The user explicitly changed the design direction after trying the
material-selection prototype. Preserve evidence/access safeguards, but do not
expose those internal processes as prerequisites to receiving an answer.

**How to apply:** Lead with a one- or two-sentence conclusion; explain briefly;
put sources under 「根拠を見る」 and reviewer metadata in a separate disclosure.
Answer general parts first, then ask at most one necessary clarification at a
time. Do not re-ask an age explicitly given in the sample.

Human guidance must explain what to discuss, distinguish counselor support from
medical judgment, and treat emergencies separately. Never imply an unconnected
service can receive a booking/message or share history without confirmation.

We育 provides parenting support, not medical consultation. Medical credentials
do not expand a counselor's in-service scope; medical judgment belongs to an
external medical pathway, without continuing a diagnostic interview here.
Honor a request for a person before the scripted conversation is complete.

**Why:** The user integrated the counselor operating model with conversation
design, explicitly rejecting AI-completion prerequisites and automatic history sharing.

**How to apply:** A handoff may summarize only what the person has already said.
In authored previews, later scripted turns are not yet confirmed facts. Keep
unknowns explicit, allow summary edits, and require recipient/content re-review
after changes; a simulated confirmation must never claim actual transmission.

Authorization for private, authored conversation samples explicitly
labeled 「編集した会話見本・実際のAI回答ではない」 does not itself authorize
model calls, automatic extraction, production canned replies, source approval,
normal-route changes, or publishing. Previous material-selection rules remain
historical prototype constraints, not a requirement for extra comparison buttons
in the new conversation-first design.

## Confirmed direction and limited refinement

Keep the conversation-first direction; make small readability changes rather
than reintroducing search forms or redesigning the consultation flow.

**Why:** The user confirmed this direction and asked for questions and answers
to be visible immediately, without a large introduction or repeated disclaimers.

**How to apply:** Keep a short editorial/AI-disconnected label. Move publisher
and recommending-body explanations into evidence unless the question directly
asks who recommends something. Keep numerical age ranges and aggregation scope
in the answer: these change its meaning and are not merely citation metadata.
Retain only question-relevant cautions. Reviewer tools and memo fields belong
on a separate surface, not inside the parent-facing preview. Verify qualifiers
against the applicable age-specific original; do not borrow adjacent-age support.

## Natural dialogue evaluation

Freeze fictional parent facts and general quality criteria before a model trial,
not a single preferred assistant answer. Grounded useful alternatives may pass.

**Why:** The user rejected stopping a trial merely because a useful response
differed from an expected method. That confounds conversational quality with
the limitations of the test script.

**How to apply:** Choose follow-ups that respond naturally within the frozen
facts; never invent an attempted result. Separate answer-quality findings,
protocol inability to continue, successful completion, and safety/transport
stops. Repetition is a quality finding, not an automatic whole-trial stop.
